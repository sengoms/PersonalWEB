WHAT THE CREDIT DOES, AND DOESN'T
The post-production credit is earned by the Georgia post-production company on its own Georgia expenditures and payroll, and it is transferable, so it can be sold for cash. Work delivered from Chennai is not a Georgia expenditure, and Georgia's vendor rules for the production credit count only services performed in Georgia. So the credit is a Year-2 lever, not a Year-1 pitch: once Hybrid VFX USA carries $100K+ of Georgia payroll (the GM plus an Atlanta compositing lead) it enters the small tier and can monetise 20% of its Georgia spend. Year-1 selling is on price, turnaround and credits. To be confirmed with a Georgia film-credit CPA before it appears in any client conversation.

05 · Go-to-Market

Four addressable segments, three networks already activated

Priority 1 is the shortest sales cycle: brands, unscripted, TV movies and independents, sold on price and overnight turnaround. VFX-house overflow runs in parallel from day one, because the Chennai facility already holds TPN Gold Shield status (valid to August 2027), the third-party assessment studios ask for on pre-release content.

Priority 1 · First revenue

Brands, agencies and unscripted

Coca-Cola, Home Depot, Delta, Chick-fil-A and UPS and their agencies; Atlanta production companies such as FUGO and REMEDY; unscripted post for Fremantle (American Idol, Family Feud), Studio Lambert (The Circle) and HGTV. Retouch, cleanup, screen comps, logo removal, versioning, overnight from Chennai. An NDA and a secure review room are all these buyers require.

Addressable: ~$25–45M/yr

Priority 1 · First revenue

Independent features and TV movies

280 productions in FY26 with independents up 83%: Lifetime and BET+ movies, Passionflix, Cineverse and the independent slate shooting in Atlanta, Athens and Savannah. 10–40 cleanup and comp shots per film, two-week post windows, flat packages, deposit first. The Atlanta post houses that finish most of them are the channel.

Addressable: ~$40–70M/yr

Priority 2 · Volume · in parallel

VFX house overflow

Crafty Apes, FuseFX, Whiskytree and SPINVFX all run Atlanta operations and service the series shot here. Roto, paint, matchmove and prep at $70–110 against $175–230 locally, delivered overnight in their pipeline. Chennai's TPN Gold Shield is the door-opener most small Indian vendors can't show.

Addressable: ~$120–180M/yr

Priority 3 · Year 2

Streamer and network series

Will Trent, Guilty Creatures, The Challenger, Heartland, Thunder Road and Tulsa King are shooting here now and award VFX to established houses. The route in is as the sub-vendor of the house that wins the show, which is what Priority 2 builds.

Addressable: ~$80–140M/yr

CONTENT SECURITY: CHENNAI HOLDS A TPN GOLD SHIELD
Studio and streamer content can only go to a TPN-listed facility, and the listing is per site: the Chennai floor, not the Atlanta review room. Hybrid's Chennai facility (Hybrid 360 Art Tech Private Limited) holds TPN Gold Shield status, completed 19 August 2025 and valid to 19 August 2027: the third-party assessment studios ask for on pre-release content, which most small Indian vendors cannot show. The renewal assessment, due before the status expires on 19 August 2027 (inside Year 1), sits with Hybrid India as facility owner; the Atlanta budget carries the review room and audit preparation so the U.S. site meets the same standard. Working target list: forty named Priority-1 targets for the first 90 days, sized at roughly 14 replies, 8 test shots and 2–3 paid pilots, which is the Month-6 gate.
