How I lead

How I lead decisions, delivery and accountability.

Clinical relevance

Start with the decision or workflow that needs to improve. Bring clinical stakeholders into the work early, and make relevance, traceability and usability part of the delivery criteria.

Accountability

Make ownership explicit across teams and partners. Establish who can authorize access, who reviews risk and who is accountable for release decisions, with controls that remain effective as the technology changes.

Decision quality

Agree on success criteria before committing to a solution. Use evaluation results to surface limitations, compare options and give leadership a clear view of readiness and unresolved risk.

Value discipline

Name the intended clinical or business outcome before investment. Assess adoption, clinician time, safety and operating cost, then use that evidence to decide where further investment is warranted.
