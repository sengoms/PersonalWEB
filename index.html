Intake and risk tiering, a living inventory of models and agents, gate criteria set before the pilot, and third-party AI assessment, mapped to the Joint Commission and CHAI responsible-use guidance, HIPAA and FDA CDS rules. How I lead

Four positions I hold, whatever the stack or the vendor.

Retrieval

Clinical questions are answered from the structured record, a terminology graph and documents together, not from documents alone. Expand clinician shorthand through the graph first, then resolve every answer to a source that carries provenance. If a system can't show the path it took, it isn't ready for a clinician.

Boundaries

The model is an untrusted caller. Anything it emits is input to be validated, never a query to be executed. Access follows the standards the ecosystem already agreed on: SMART on FHIR scopes for minimum-necessary data, OAuth 2.1 and MCP authorization for tool servers, and a FHIR AuditEvent so every access is recorded.

Evidence

Define the gate before the pilot: task benchmarks, citation precision and recall, a synthetic-conflict patient set, and an adversarial corpus with a zero-leakage target. Evaluation harnesses turn model choice from a vendor conversation into a measurement, and give leadership a clear view of readiness and unresolved risk.

Value

Name the clinical or business outcome before the pilot starts, and decide then what evidence would retire it. Measure clinician time returned, safety events and adoption alongside dollars, because hard financial return on clinical AI lags by years. Scale what clears the gate and stop what doesn't. A portfolio of pilots is a cost, not a strategy.

How a use case gets from concept to production.

The same six steps for every clinical AI use case, whatever the vendor or model. The gate is decided before the pilot, and missing it is a result, not a failure.

01Concept

Name the clinical or business outcome, and what evidence would retire the idea.

02Intake & risk tier

Into the inventory with an owner. The tier sets the depth of review, so low-risk work moves fast.

03Pilot

Built on the shared platform, inside the security boundary, not beside it.

04The gate

Task benchmarks · citation precision & recall · synthetic-conflict set · adversarial corpus, zero leakage.

05Production

Scoped access, an audit record on every call, monitoring from day one.

06Measure, or stop

Clinician time, safety events, adoption, cost. Miss the gate, stop the pilot.
