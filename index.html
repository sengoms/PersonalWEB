Selected work

Three pieces of work, described by what they decided rather than what they used.

Clinical AI retrieval platform
Mayo Clinic, 2026
Architecture & design review lead

A clinically grounded question-answering platform over a longitudinal patient record. I designed the hybrid retrieval architecture (FHIR store, clinical knowledge graph, document vectors with graph-first routing), the nine-tool MCP pipeline for cited answers, and the security boundary — then led the vendor design review and wrote the CTO-facing design and delivery plan.

  • Closed the LLM-to-graph injection class by design: five typed, parameter-bound traversal templates replace free-form Cypher; guideline logic lives in CPG-on-FHIR and CQL, evaluated in code.
  • Resolved a four-way leadership disagreement on service boundaries with a decision record — agents orchestrate, servers guarantee — that kept minimum-necessary, audit and non-device CDS posture intact.
  • Defined the pilot gate: agent benchmarks, citation precision and recall, a synthetic-conflict patient set, and an adversarial corpus with zero-leakage targets.
Clinical knowledge graph
Mayo Clinic, 2024–25
Design & production

A production Neo4j graph of SNOMED CT and ICD-10-CM with IS_A, FINDING_SITE and CAUSATIVE_AGENT relationships, a SNOMED-to-ICD crossmap, and sub-100 ms term expansion that turns “MI” into its full ontology footprint. In controlled evaluation, ontology-grounded retrieval measurably outperformed the vector-only baseline — the reproducible result that secured executive investment.

Enterprise GenAI platform
Mayo Clinic, 2023–25
Architecture & production hardening

An NVIDIA RAG Blueprint deployment on GKE with a six-phase evaluation framework and Go/No-Go gates; a provider-agnostic LLM layer with zero-downtime model swaps; a multi-agent pipeline routing cited answers across enterprise sources; and an MCP tool server proposed as the enterprise standard for AI tool interoperability. Promoted across four environments with dual-region HA, IAP/OAuth2, VPC Service Controls and Vault — to formal security sign-off.
